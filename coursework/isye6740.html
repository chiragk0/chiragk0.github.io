ISYE 6740 Computational Data Analytics

Georgia Tech · Fall 2025

Core machine learning: clustering, dimensionality reduction, density estimation, classification, ensembles and change-point detection.

PythonNumPyscikit-learnMatplotlibLaTeX

What I covered

  • Clustering. K-means with squared-L2 and Manhattan distances (applied to image compression), choosing k, and spectral clustering on a network.
  • Dimensionality reduction. PCA derived from the covariance eigenproblem, ISOMAP for ordering face images, and eigenfaces for simple face recognition.
  • Density estimation and mixtures. Kernel density estimation and the EM algorithm for Gaussian mixture models, implemented from scratch.
  • Classification. SVMs, AdaBoost and random forests compared on MNIST digits and Fashion-MNIST, plus a random forest and one-class SVM spam classifier.
  • Sparse recovery and regression. Lasso and Ridge for compressed sensing and medical-image reconstruction, and locally weighted linear regression.
  • Change-point detection. A CUSUM statistic for detecting shifts in a data stream.
  • Neural networks. Backpropagation derived and implemented by hand.
