ISYE 6644 Simulation

Georgia Tech · Summer 2026

Discrete-event and Monte Carlo simulation: building models, generating random inputs, and analyzing outputs statistically.

PythonNumPySciPyArenaSpreadsheet simulation

What I covered

  • Simulation foundations. Hand and spreadsheet simulations, general simulation principles, and discrete-event modeling in Arena.
  • Random number and variate generation. Generating uniform random numbers and transforming them into draws from other distributions.
  • Input and output analysis. Fitting input distributions to data, and analyzing simulation output with confidence intervals.
  • Comparing systems. Statistical procedures for comparing alternative system designs.
  • Course project. A Monte Carlo study of the newsvendor problem (see Projects).
