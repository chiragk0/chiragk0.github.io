CSE 6242 Data and Visual Analytics

Georgia Tech

The full analytics pipeline: acquiring and cleaning data, storing it, modeling it, and visualizing it.

PythonSQL (SQLite)D3.jsTableauFlaskSpark / DatabricksOpenRefine

What I covered

  • Data acquisition and wrangling. Collecting data from the TMDb API to build a co-actor network, cleaning with OpenRefine, and storing and querying with SQLite.
  • Visualization. Designing tables and charts in Tableau, and building D3 force-directed graphs, line charts, interactive views and choropleth maps.
  • Web applications. Serving data and visualizations from a small Flask app.
  • Scalable computation. PageRank and personalized PageRank implementation, and large-scale data processing in Spark.
  • Machine learning. Random forest implementation and model comparison with scikit-learn, including feature importance and hyperparameter tuning.
