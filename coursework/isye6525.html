ISYE 6525 Topics on High-Dimensional Data Analytics

Georgia Tech · Spring 2026

Image processing and large-scale optimization methods for high-dimensional data.

PythonNumPyOpenCV / scikit-imageMatplotlibLaTeX

What I covered

  • Image processing. Point transformations and histogram operations, noise and convolution-based denoising, Gaussian low- and high-pass filters, and hybrid images.
  • Segmentation and edges. Otsu thresholding, K-means color segmentation, and gradient-based edge detection with Sobel and Prewitt operators.
  • Constrained and splitting methods. Deriving and implementing ADMM, including the augmented Lagrangian and the closed-form updates.
  • Coordinate descent. Closed-form coordinate updates for ridge and elastic-net regression.
  • Proximal gradient. Proximal gradient descent for elastic-net-regularized logistic regression.
