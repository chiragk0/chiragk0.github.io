CS 7280 Network Science

Georgia Tech · Fall 2025

Graph structure, spreading processes and network growth, with hands-on analysis of empirical networks.

PythonNetworkXEoN (Epidemics on Networks)Jupyter

What I covered

  • Graph fundamentals. Building graphs with prescribed properties, degree statistics, spectral features of the adjacency matrix, and weighted networks.
  • Degree structure. The friendship paradox and power-law (scale-free) degree distributions, tested on empirical networks.
  • Centrality and communities. Centrality metrics and community detection methods compared on real network data.
  • Epidemic modeling. SIS outbreaks simulated on a Dublin contact network with EoN, comparing empirical time constants and endemic sizes with theory.
  • Robustness and growth. Network robustness and percolation under node removal, and preferential attachment growth models.
